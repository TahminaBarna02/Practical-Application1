# Practical-Application1
Will a customer accept the coupon?
In this project, I explored how customer behavior and driving conditions affect coupon acceptance. I found that customers are more likely to accept coupons
that match their interests and habits. For example, people who visit bars more often are more likely to accept bar coupons. Other factors, such as age,
income, and passengers, may also play a role. Overall, this project helped me understand how customer preferences can influence coupon acceptance.
overall, this project analyzes customer behavior and driving conditions to identify the factors that influence whether customers accept coupons.
